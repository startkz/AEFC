# IoT-J 76369 — verified six-experiment response update

This document replaces the pending evidence in `IOTJ_Six_Experimental_Rebuttal_Addendum_WORKING.md`. The full response including all 28 comments is a separate manuscript artifact; this addendum is scoped to the six experiments.

**AE1/R1.1/R2.2: independent recovery-advantage weighting.** In 60/60 complete 1000-episode, 200-round and 10-held-out-test full-budget runs (10 paired training seeds per method/case, matched 40,230,000 application bytes), IEEE39 AEFC minus Random is +1.1909 pp (paired 95% bootstrap [+0.0546,+3.0333]), minus TD-error +1.0305 pp ([+0.2103,+1.9928]), and minus Uniform-High-Advantage **-0.1940 pp** ([-0.5352,+0.0736]). Uniform weighting of the same positive-advantage transitions is *not* demonstrated inferior to numeric advantage weights. IEEE118 saturates at 100% for all modes. Evidence: https://github.com/startkz/AEFC/actions/runs/41407429811 and base https://github.com/startkz/AEFC/actions/runs/41390041858.

**AE2/R1.3/R2.3: collaborative M/N metadata poisoning.** All 60/60 1000-episode attack runs and 10/10 M0/N10 controls were independently verified. On IEEE39 the clean M0/N10 safe rate is 20/50. Under original aggregation, M=1/2/3 jointly forging update, utility and prototype all yield **0/50** safe tests; their mean coalition weight masses are 0.6578/0.5738/0.6580. With a separately labeled per-client max-weight 0.20 variant, safe tests are 10/50, 0/50 and 0/50, respectively. The cap does not bind the whole coalition to 0.20; worst-case Byzantine robustness is unproven. Evidence: https://github.com/startkz/AEFC/actions/runs/41407433389 and https://github.com/startkz/AEFC/actions/runs/41408532307.

**R1.2/R1.9: same-version No-Prototype rerun and semantic alignment.** Twelve of twelve full-budget paired prototype-on/off cases were independently verified. IEEE39 safe tests are 10/30 (with prototype) vs 29/30 (without), and paired terminal-load difference is **-3.4148 pp** (n=3 seeds, exploratory bootstrap [-3.7959,-2.7448]). Separate same-scoped semantic diagnostics show mean local/global prototype L2 0.1332 vs 27.7601 and cosine 0.9971 vs 0.5743 (on/off). Stronger representation alignment does not translate into better physical recovery in this case. Two historical semantic jobs originally failed only a floating-point `100.00000000000001>100` assertion; all 12 original JSON results passed immutable independent re-audit without retraining. Evidence: https://github.com/startkz/AEFC/actions/runs/41407437933 and https://github.com/startkz/AEFC/actions/runs/41452265680.

**AE4/R1.7/R2.5: enforceable physical constraint filtering.** A frozen, full-budget-trained IEEE39 policy (seed82) underwent identical 10×30-step held-out AC evaluation with a prospective least-intervention feasible-action filter on/off. Five-step safe tests were 10/10 on vs 1/10 off; AC-unsafe steps 136 vs 291; 138 actions were modified. IEEE118 had 10/10 safe tests and no intervention under either setting. This is a validated **quasi-static AC voltage and thermal** execution filter, not dynamic-frequency, hardware-in-the-loop or formal invariance validation. Evidence: https://github.com/startkz/AEFC/actions/runs/41409094153.

**R1.10: non-oracle traffic plus exact payload accounting.** The attack-status bit was removed from the policy observation of a two-phase queue PoC, and 15 independent seeds/method were run. Mean queue / recovery success / serialized application upload: AEFC PoC 38.112/23.573%/14,400 bytes, FedRL 39.533/36.213%/17,600, FedRL-Clip 24.278/54.187%/17,600. AEFC transmits 18.18% fewer bytes but **underperforms FedRL-Clip**. This traffic PoC is not the full grid PER AEFC learner; network headers and downlink are excluded. Evidence: https://github.com/startkz/AEFC/actions/runs/41407990103.

**Privacy/MIA: source-label independent attack evidence.** On each grid, five independently trained full-budget target models were tested with three attack seeds/target, 128 members and 128 nonmembers per attack, and an attacker trained on shadow clients 0/2 and evaluated against target client 4. All labels and prediction scores were archived; all 30 AUCs were independently recomputed. IEEE39 AUC=0.52100 (SD 0.05084); IEEE118 AUC=0.51187 (SD 0.03841). The attacker sees the *local* actor, encoder, critic and prototypes; these observations **do not** establish privacy against arbitrary server-only attackers, DP, or an information-theoretic bound. Evidence: https://github.com/startkz/AEFC/actions/runs/41407717862.

**Audit provenance:** 142 new complete training-level raw ZIP archives were checked against model SHA256, 1000 episode records, 200 synchronization records, real serialized application-byte sums, and ten 30-step held-out tests each. Checks passed for all 142. The 30 MIA AUCs were recomputed from 7680 original labels/scores. Exact source SHA, experimental group summaries and seed-level uncertainty are described in `docs/IOTJ_Six_Reviewer_Experiments_Verified_20261102.md` and in the independent Chinese working report.

**Unresolved prior editorial requirements:** faithful A2FC [59] and predecessor [40] adapted baseline implementations; dynamic IEEE39/118 generator frequency and actual protection; source-matched server-only MIA; full network wire bytes; reconciliation of old plotted values to final clean/marked manuscript. No statements of unconditional safety or universal superiority are supported by the present results.
